Press & Media

Tyler's Tandem is a creator-led charity fundraiser riding a tandem bike down the entire California coast with strangers to raise money for mental health. We welcome press coverage and partnership inquiries.

About the campaign

Tyler Mitic, a content creator based in California, is riding a tandem bike from the Oregon border to the Mexican border — over 1,000 miles — picking up strangers as co-riders along the way. Every mile is dedicated to mental health awareness and fundraising for Bring Change 2 Mind.

The campaign features daily video content posted to @tylermitic, live donation tracking, and a dare system where donors can unlock challenges for Tyler to complete on camera.

About Bring Change 2 Mind

Bring Change 2 Mind is a nonprofit organization founded to end the stigma and discrimination surrounding mental illness. All donations raised through Tyler's Tandem go directly to BC2M to fund mental health programs and awareness campaigns.

Learn more at bringchange2mind.org
